# PowderMeet achievement badges

The 42 badges and the art direction their pictures were made from (22 Nov 2026).
The rules the app applies are in `packages/AppCore/Sources/AppCore/Badges.swift` and
`specs/08-ACTIVITY.md` section 7: 26 are earned today, from the run history and the
meets reached, and the rest show as "Soon". What is left is in `docs/ROADMAP.md`, Badges.

## Art direction, to paste before any badge

> A set of ski achievement badges for an iPhone app called PowderMeet. Every badge is
> the same shape and size: a hexagonal enamel pin seen straight on, with a thin
> brushed-metal rim, on a transparent background, 1024 by 1024 pixels, centred, no
> text, no letters, no numbers, no drop shadow outside the pin. Flat, bold, simple
> shapes that still read at 44 pixels. Palette: PowderMeet red (#E8262A), white,
> near-black (#0B0B0D) and one accent colour named per badge. Each badge comes in
> three tiers that differ only in the rim: bronze, silver, gold. Keep the style
> identical from badge to badge so they look like one collection.

Ask for one badge at a time, with the art direction first, then the badge's line.
Where a badge has tiers, ask for the three rims in one image, side by side.
